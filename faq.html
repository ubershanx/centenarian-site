Getting started

What is Centenarian?

An AI habit coach that lives in WhatsApp. The aim is more healthy years, not just a longer life. You start with what you want to still be able to do at 80 or 90, set a goal for the next 100 days, and work back to one to three tiny daily habits. Each evening I check in once and you tap done or not today.

Who can use it?

Anyone 18 or older who has been invited. Centenarian is invite-only for now, mainly for friends and family in India and Singapore. If someone invited you, just message the number they gave you. If not, you can join the waiting list on offscript.cloud.

How do I start, and how long does it take?

Say hi on WhatsApp. I'll explain how your data is kept and ask you to agree, then we talk through who you want to be later in life, a short check-up of where you are today, a 100-day goal and the habits that serve it. It takes about 10 to 12 minutes, mostly taps, and you can stop and pick up where you left off within 7 days.

Do I have to tap the buttons?

No. Buttons are shortcuts. You can always answer in your own words, and ask me questions about your habits or healthy living at any point.

How do I see what I can ask for?

Send "help" (or "faq") any time for a short list of things you can do, and a link to this page.

Why does this approach work?

It's built on two ideas: tiny habits tied to things you already do, which grow a little when you're on track (from James Clear's Atomic Habits), and daily protocols for sleep, light, movement, stress and food (from Andrew Huberman's work). Send "learn" for short lessons, each under a minute to read.

Which languages does it speak?

English only for now. Please type rather than send voice notes: I can't listen to them yet.

Habits and check-ins

How many habits can I have?

Up to three in a season. A few habits done most days beat many done sometimes. If you want to add another, I'll offer to swap one out instead.

What is the 2-minute version?

Every habit has a tiny version for busy days, like putting on your walking shoes and stepping outside. Tapping "2-min version" counts as done for your consistency, though not as meeting a growing target.

When does the check-in come, and can I change it?

Once a day at the time you chose (8:30 pm unless you picked another). To move it, just tell me, for example "check in at 9:15 pm". You can also tell me a habit is done before then, like "done walk", and the evening check-in only asks about the rest.

What is the morning message?

A short good-morning with today's habits and one idea on why they matter, at 7:30 am unless you change it. Send "morning 6:45" to move it, "morning off" to stop it, or "morning on" to bring it back.

What if I miss a day?

One miss is normal. Tomorrow, even the 2-minute version counts. If you don't answer at all, that day counts as unknown, not missed, and you can still tell me within 48 hours ("yesterday I did all three"). Your headline number is consistency, not a streak, and nothing ever resets to zero.

Can I pause while I'm ill or travelling?

Yes. Send "pause" and say for how long: up to 7 days at a time and 14 days in a season. Paused days don't count against you. The day count keeps running, and check-ins start again on their own.

Why does my target change each week?

Some habits, like daily steps, grow a little when you're on track. Every Sunday I look at your week: a good week moves the target up a step, a mixed week holds it, and a hard week eases it back to rebuild momentum. It never goes past a safe target. Reply "keep it here" if you'd rather stay where you are for another week.

Can I send a screenshot from my fitness app?

Yes. Send a screenshot of your steps or sleep and I'll read the numbers and ask before recording them against your check-in. The image itself isn't kept. Up to 5 a day.

How do I stop reminders?

Reply STOP any time and I'll only reply when you message me. Send START to turn them back on. If you don't answer for a few days I check in less often, and after 7 quiet days I stop on my own. Your season stays open: just message me when you're back.

Seasons and goals

What is a season?

100 days of tiny daily habits towards one goal, with milestones at Days 21, 50, 75 and 100. At each milestone you get a summary of how each habit is going.

Can I change my habits?

Before Day 1, send CHANGE to choose again. Once the season has started, you can add a goal of your own (if you already have three habits, I'll ask which one it replaces, and your check-ins so far are kept), or restart or start a new season.

What's the difference between RESTART and NEW SEASON?

RESTART keeps the same goal and habits, takes them back to your starting levels, and makes tomorrow Day 1. NEW SEASON starts fresh: new goal, new habits, and you choose which old habits to keep. Either way your history stays, nothing changes until you confirm, and a restart is reinforcing, not starting over.

What happens after Day 100?

You get a Day 100 summary, and you can start a new season: keep the habits that stuck and pick new ones to focus on, or repeat a season to reinforce it. Starting a season never depends on bloodwork or a scorecard.

Can I ask for my own goal or habit?

Yes. Tell me in your own words, like "I want to do cold showers". I first check it's safe to coach, then look into the research, check every study I cite really exists, and come back in a few minutes with a habit, its evidence grade and a 2-minute version.

What do the evidence grades mean?

They show how strong the research behind a habit is, from A (strongest) to D. A habit I researched for you is marked "auto-researched, not yet reviewed" until our team reviews it. If the evidence isn't there, I can track it as your own habit, with no health claims.

Is there a limit on research?

Up to 5 new research requests a month. A habit someone has already researched doesn't count. Past the limit, I can track it as your own habit, or you can ask again next month.

Why did you turn down my goal?

Some goals could do more harm than good, or need someone who knows your health, so I won't coach them. I'll suggest a gentler habit where I can, and it's worth talking the goal over with your doctor.

Health records (the vault)

What is the health vault?

A private place for your health papers. Send a photo or PDF of a vaccination card, prescription, lab report or discharge summary on WhatsApp, and I keep the full file, read its key details (type, date, who it's for, medicines, vaccines or results, as written) and organise it by person.

Do I have to agree to anything first?

One consent, given when you start, covers your health records, so there's nothing more to agree to. If you agreed to an earlier version, I ask you once, with the same short note, before keeping your first record. If you say no, the file isn't kept and your coaching carries on as before.

What can I send?

Photos (JPEG or PNG) and PDFs, up to 20 records a day. Large scans are fine. If I can't read one clearly, I still keep it as an unread record. If you send the same record twice, I keep just the first.

How do you know whose record it is?

I read the name printed on it and suggest who it's for. Nothing is kept until you choose. If it lands with the wrong person, tap "Wrong person" and it moves, with its lab values and reminders.

How do I see my records?

Send "my records". I'll send a private link that works for 7 days. Each time you open it, I send a one-time code here on WhatsApp. On the page you can view, download, correct or delete records, see lab values over time, and download everything as a zip.

Can I keep records for my family?

Yes, for up to 6 people. An adult agrees on their own WhatsApp through a link you forward them, unless you're their lawful guardian. A child's records need a parent's declaration on your private page first. A family member can send STOP RECORDS from their own number any time to have their records deleted.

Can it remind me about vaccine doses?

Yes. If a vaccination card states the next due date, I set a reminder for it and tell you. If it doesn't, I ask whether you'd like one and when. The date always comes from the card or from you, never from me.

What do you do with lab results?

I keep each result as printed, with its reference range, and show every value of a measure across your reports with a simple trend chart. If a report flags a value, I say so and suggest discussing it with your doctor. I never explain results or say whether a trend is good or bad.

Longevity scorecard

What is the longevity scorecard?

Every three months, a short list of things linked to a long healthy life: a few blood markers, blood pressure, weight, waist-to-height, and three simple fitness tests you can do at home. It shows how many measured lines are inside your reports' ranges and which way each is moving. There's no single score. Send SCORECARD to start or update it (about 3 minutes), and MY SCORECARD to see it.

Do I need a blood test for it?

No. A scorecard with three lines is fine, and missing bloodwork never blocks anything, including starting a season. If a lab report in your vault is from the last three months, it fills the blood lines for you. About 15 days before your quarter ends, I'll list any blood tests still missing.

Whose ranges does it use?

Only the reference ranges printed on your own lab reports. Lines without a printed range, like weight or fitness tests, show their trend only. Anything outside a range is marked neutrally with "discuss with your doctor".

Are the fitness tests safe for me?

Before your first fitness tests I ask a few yes/no safety questions. Any yes means the tests are skipped, with a note to discuss them with your doctor first, and they never count against you. Stop any test if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.

Can I track my own measures?

Yes, up to 5 of your own lines: a lab measure you watch, or something you name, like a rowing time. Send SCORECARD ADD to add one, or SCORECARD REMOVE to drop one.

Privacy and your data

Where is my data kept?

Encrypted, in Mumbai, India. Your phone number is stored separately from everything else.

Who can see my data?

Only you see your health records. While Centenarian is invite-only, the owner can see activity, chosen habits and consistency under a code, never your name or number, and never your health records or safety answers. Requests and feedback you send are passed on with names and numbers removed, and a few messages, also with names and numbers removed, may be reviewed to improve replies.

Does an AI read my messages?

Yes. An AI provider (Anthropic) reads your messages, and each health record once, so I can reply and organise them. It never uses them to train AI, and your phone number is never sent to it.

How do I delete my data?

Send "delete my data". I'll ask you to confirm, then permanently delete everything about you, including your health records. It can't be undone.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. On your private records page you can download your records as a zip and lab values as a spreadsheet. For anything else, ask in the chat or email privacy@centenarian.in.

Can I share my progress with a friend? (coming)

Not yet. Sharing with accountability partners is planned. When it comes, you'll choose what each person sees, and health records, lab values and messages are never shared.

Where is the full privacy notice?

At https://centenarian.in/privacy.html.

Costs and limits

Does it cost anything?

No. Centenarian is free during the invite-only phase, and nothing is ever charged without your clear agreement.

Is there a limit on chatting with the coach?

Each person has a monthly AI allowance; setting up your first season doesn't use it. You'll get a heads-up at about 80%. Past it, buttons and check-ins keep working, and free-text coaching waits until the next month.

Will WhatsApp charge me?

Centenarian doesn't charge you for messages. Your mobile data plan applies as for any WhatsApp chat.

Safety and medical questions

Is Centenarian a doctor?

No. I'm an AI habit coach. I can't diagnose, prescribe, or advise on medicines or doses. For anything about your health, medicines or test results, please talk to your doctor or pharmacist.

I have a health condition or take medication. Can I still use it?

Yes, but talk to your doctor before changing your diet or exercise. Some habits come with a few yes/no questions first; any yes means I won't suggest that habit and recommend discussing it with your doctor. I keep only the answer, never details.

What if I tell you about an emergency?

If a message sounds like an emergency, such as chest pain or thoughts of self-harm, I stop coaching and point you to emergency help. Centenarian is not an emergency service: if you need urgent help, call your local emergency number.

Can I use it while pregnant?

Please check with your doctor before changing your diet or exercise while pregnant.

Will these habits improve my health?

I can't promise a health result. I show how strong the research behind each habit is and say what it's "linked to", and the rest is showing up most days.

Centenarian is an AI habit coach, not a doctor, and does not give medical advice. See also the privacy notice.
