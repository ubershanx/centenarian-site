Privacy notice

Version 0.3 (health records and pre-registration added) · Last updated 25 November 2026 · Applies to the invite-only phase

Centenarian is a habit coach you use inside WhatsApp. It is run by Shankar Ramchandran (“we”). This notice explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and how you can get a copy or delete it. We wrote it to match exactly how the app works.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to coach your habits.
  • Your data is stored in Mumbai, India, and encrypted at rest.
  • We never sell your data and never use it to train AI models.
  • Send “delete my data” in the chat at any time and we delete everything.
  • Centenarian is an AI habit coach, not a doctor, and does not give medical advice.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number, stored encrypted in one place only, so we can reply to you.
  • Your messages to the app, so it can understand and answer them.
  • Your habits and check-ins: the habits you choose, your cues, your daily answers, and your progress.
  • Basic settings: country, time zone, check-in time, and (only if a habit needs it) whether you are under 60 or 60+.
  • Safety screening answers (yes/no only) for habits that could harm some people. These are never shared with anyone.
  • Your consent: when you agreed and to which version of this notice.

We do not ask for your name, exact age, location or contacts. Health records are kept only if you choose to use the health vault, with its own consent (below).

Health records (the health vault)

If you agree to it separately, you can send the app photos or PDFs of health records (for example vaccination cards, prescriptions, lab reports or discharge summaries), for yourself or for family members you look after.

  • What we keep: the full file, and what is read from it: the type of record, its date, the name on it, and the vaccines, medicines or test results it lists, as written.
  • Where: encrypted by the app and stored in Google Cloud in Mumbai, India; the details read from it are stored encrypted in our database in Mumbai.
  • Who reads it: our AI provider (Anthropic) reads each file once, so the app can organise it; it is not used to train AI. After that, only you can see your records, on a private page reached by a link and a one-time code sent to your WhatsApp. The app owner cannot see them.
  • No medical advice: the app only restates what a record says. It does not interpret results, judge doses or suggest changes.
  • Family members: an adult's records are kept only after that adult agrees on their own WhatsApp, or after you confirm you are their lawful guardian. They can withdraw at any time by sending STOP RECORDS, and their records are deleted. A child's records (under 18) are kept only after a parent or lawful guardian makes a declaration on the private page; they are used only to keep those records.
  • Your control: view, download, correct or delete any record, or a family member's profile, on your private page; download everything as a zip; "delete my data" deletes all of it.

Pre-registration (the waiting list)

If you sign up for an invite on offscript.cloud, Centenarian keeps your name, email and WhatsApp number, only so it can invite you when there's a place.

  • Where: encrypted, in Centenarian's database in Mumbai, India. The Offscript site only shows the form; it keeps no copy.
  • Who sees it: the app owner, to choose whom to invite. When you join, these details are deleted (the app then keeps only what's described above).
  • Messages: one WhatsApp message when your place is ready, and at most one reminder a week later. Nothing else.
  • How long: up to 12 months on the waiting list, then deleted.
  • Leaving: reply STOP to the invite, or email privacy@centenarian.in; we delete your entry within 7 days.

Why we use it

  • To run your habit program: reminders, check-ins, progress and coaching replies.
  • To keep you safe: if a message suggests an emergency, the app stops coaching and points you to help.
  • To control costs fairly: we record what each account costs to run.
  • To improve the service, as described in “Who can see your data” below.

Who can see your data

  • Accountability partners see nothing unless you choose to share, and even then only simple signals you pick (for example, whether you checked in). They never see your messages, screening answers or health information.
  • The app owner (invite-only phase): can see each user’s activity, chosen habits and consistency under a code instead of your name, to understand whether the app is working. The owner cannot see your messages, health records or screening answers on the dashboard.
  • The app owner (after public launch): only combined statistics across many users.
  • Quality checks: a small sample of messages, with names and numbers removed, may be reviewed to test and improve the app’s replies.

Services that process your data for us

We use these providers only to run the app, under their business terms:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between you and the app.
  • Anthropic (Claude API): the AI that reads some messages to understand and reply. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, API data is not used to train its models. We send only what is needed for a reply, never your phone number.
  • Google Cloud (Mumbai region): runs the app and stores health-vault files, encrypted.
  • Supabase (Mumbai region): stores the app’s database.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: while you use the app, until you delete it.
  • Health records: until you delete them (or the family member withdraws); a deleted file is gone at once, with no copies kept.
  • Usage events (for statistics): up to 13 months, then only combined totals remain.
  • Cost records: kept for accounting, with the link to you removed when you delete your data.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: send “delete my data”. We confirm, then delete your account, messages, habits and usage events.
  • Copy: ask for an export of your data in the chat.
  • Stop reminders: reply STOP at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: deleting your data withdraws it.
  • You can also contact us at privacy@centenarian.in.

We aim to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

Who can use Centenarian

You must be 18 or older. During the invite-only phase, only people invited by the owner can use it. A parent or lawful guardian may keep a child's health records in the health vault, as described above.

Not medical advice

Centenarian suggests everyday habits and shows the research behind them. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace your doctor. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or take medication, talk to your doctor before changing your diet or exercise.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that affects you, we will tell you in WhatsApp and, where needed, ask for your consent again.
